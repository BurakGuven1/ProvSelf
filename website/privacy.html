Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ProvSelf ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the ProvSelf mobile application and related website.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email, username, and profile details.
  • Challenge and progress data, including check-ins and optional notes.
  • Optional proof data (photo and/or Apple Health metrics) when you submit verification.
  • Device and app diagnostics needed for security, bug fixing, and reliability.

How We Use Information

  • Provide challenge creation, verification, reminders, and social accountability features.
  • Protect accounts, prevent abuse, and maintain platform integrity.
  • Improve app quality, performance, and user experience.
  • Respond to support and legal/privacy requests.

Health and Photo Data

If you connect Apple Health, we access only metrics required for your selected challenge verification flow. Photo proof is used strictly for challenge verification and anti-abuse logic.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with service providers that help us operate the app (such as infrastructure, analytics, and customer support), under contractual safeguards.

Data Retention

We retain data as long as needed to provide services, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You can request account deletion by contacting support.

Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@provself.app.

Children

ProvSelf is not directed to children under the age required by applicable local laws.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: privacy@provself.app
